# Wealth Management Product Discovery

## At a glance

- **Brief:** Define a new wealth-management section within an existing fintech app.
- **Role:** Product Lead / Product Manager
- **Ownership:** Discovery, MVP, flows, requirements, backlog and cross-functional alignment.
- **Outcome:** User story map, roadmap and clickable high-fidelity prototype.

## Context and challenge

The client wanted to broaden its original investment proposition inside an existing app. The key question was how to bring several product types into one clear structure without making the experience unnecessarily complex.

## My role

I led the product side of the discovery. I clarified the opportunity and first-version boundaries, prepared the story map, flows and requirements, worked on the prototype, and translated the proposal into a roadmap and backlog.

## Outcome

The outputs created a shared foundation for further decisions and delivery. The client and public URL are omitted for confidentiality.

## Reflection

The important part was not adding as many features as possible, but finding a framework in which different investment products could feel like one coherent experience.

# Mobile Investment App

## At a glance

- **Brief:** Extend a web investment platform into native iOS and Android applications.
- **Role:** Product Owner
- **Ownership:** Discovery, MVP, user flows, user stories, backlog and delivery alignment.
- **Outcome:** The app shipped on schedule and received Apple and Google approval.

## Context and challenge

Across had an established web investment platform and wanted to extend it into a full mobile product. The app had to make a complex investment, custody and compliance setup work on mobile, stay consistent with the web platform and pass store review within a fixed timeline.

## My role

I helped define and continuously refine the product strategy. I led discovery and delivery for the mobile app and related enhancements to the web platform. I defined the MVP and key flows, translated the direction into user stories, managed the backlog, and aligned decisions across business, design and engineering.

## Outcome

We shipped on schedule and received approval from Apple and Google.

## Reflection

The hardest part was simplifying a regulated investment product for mobile without losing essential information or continuity with the web platform.
